Q1 · JPHG02.2 · Standard

An 800 W electric heater is switched on for 3 minutes. How much electrical energy does it use? (E = Pt)

  • A. 2,400 J
  • B. 48,000 J
  • C. 144,000 J
  • D. 1,440,000 J

Q2 · JPHG02.3 · Standard

A 0.30 kg sample of water is heated from 25°C to 65°C. How much energy does it gain? (E = mcΔT)

Specific heat capacity of water = 4,200 J/(kg°C)

  • A. 5,040 J
  • B. 12,600 J
  • C. 50,400 J
  • D. 504,000 J

Q3 · JPHG02.4 · Standard

A 0.40 kg metal block absorbs 18,000 J of energy. Its temperature rises by 50°C. What is its specific heat capacity? Assume no energy is lost.

  • A. 90 J/(kg°C)
  • B. 360 J/(kg°C)
  • C. 900 J/(kg°C)
  • D. 2,250 J/(kg°C)

Q4 · JPHG02.6 · Standard

A 0.28 kg aluminium block at 100°C is placed in 0.18 kg of water at 20°C in an insulated container. What is their final temperature? Ignore energy absorbed by the container.

Specific heat capacity of aluminium = 900 J/(kg°C)

Specific heat capacity of water = 4,200 J/(kg°C)

  • A. 30°C
  • B. 40°C
  • C. 60°C
  • D. 80°C

JPHG02 Heat and internal energy — Quiz 1 · Answer key

Teacher page · 4 items

Q1 · JPHG02.2

Answer: C. 144,000 J

Guide: Time is 3 × 60 = 180 s. E = Pt = 800 W × 180 s = 144,000 J.

Q2 · JPHG02.3

Answer: C. 50,400 J

Guide: ΔT = 65°C − 25°C = 40°C. E = mcΔT = 0.30 kg × 4,200 J/(kg°C) × 40°C = 50,400 J.

Q3 · JPHG02.4

Answer: C. 900 J/(kg°C)

Guide: c = E / (mΔT) = 18,000 J / (0.40 kg × 50°C) = 900 J/(kg°C).

Q4 · JPHG02.6

Answer: B. 40°C

Guide: Energy lost by the aluminium equals energy gained by the water: 0.28 × 900 × (100 − T) = 0.18 × 4,200 × (T − 20). This gives 252(100 − T) = 756(T − 20), so T = 40°C.
