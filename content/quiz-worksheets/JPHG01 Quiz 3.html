S3 Physics · 3 multiple-choice items

Q1 · JPHG01.2 · Standard

The resistance of the metal coil in a resistance thermometer is 70 Ω at 30 °C and 80 Ω at 50 °C. What is the resistance of the metal coil if it is placed in boiling water? Assume that the resistance of the coil varies linearly with temperature.

  • A. 105 Ω
  • B. 150 Ω
  • C. 155 Ω
  • D. 205 Ω

Q2 · JPHG01.3 · Foundation

A mercury-in-glass thermometer will response to temperature change more quickly if

  • A. the inner diameter of the tube is reduced.
  • B. the thickness of the wall is reduced.
  • C. the size of the bulb is increased.
  • D. the length of the tube is increased.

Q3 · JPHG01.3 · Standard

The graphs below show the variation of the electrical resistance R of four circuit elements with temperature T. Which of the circuit elements is the most suitable for measuring temperature?

  • A. R increases with T, and the graph curves upwards.
  • B. R increases with T, and the graph curves downwards.
  • C. R increases linearly with T.
  • D. R does not change with T.

JPHG01 Temperature and thermometer — Quiz 3 · Answer key

Teacher page · 3 items

Q1 · JPHG01.2

Answer: A. 105 Ω

Guide: Let the resistance of the metal coil at 100 °C be x. (x − 70)/(80 − 70) = (100 − 30)/(50 − 30) x = 105 Ω.

Q2 · JPHG01.3

Answer: B. the thickness of the wall is reduced.

Q3 · JPHG01.3

Answer: C. R increases linearly with T.

Guide: The electrical resistance R of the circuit element varies linearly with the temperature T. The thermometer making use of the variation of R can have a linear scale which is the most suitable.
