Which of the following thermometers measures temperature by detecting the intensity of radiation?

  • A. Liquid-in-glass thermometer
  • B. Resistance thermometer

Q2 · JPHG01.1 · Foundation

Which of the following statements about the Celsius temperature scale is/are correct?

(1) The lower fixed point is the lowest temperature existing in nature.

(2) The upper fixed point is the temperature of steam.

(3) The unit of this scale is °C.

  • A. (1) only
  • B. (3) only
  • C. (1) and (2) only
  • D. (2) and (3) only

Q3 · JPHG01.2 · Standard

A faulty thermometer with uniform scale reads 5 °C and 95 °C when it is placed in melting ice and boiling water respectively. What is the actual temperature if the thermometer reads 30 °C?

  • A. 27.8 °C
  • B. 28.5 °C
  • C. 30 °C
  • D. 35 °C

JPHG01 Temperature and thermometer — Quiz 1 · Answer key

Teacher page · 3 items

Q1 · JPHG01.3

Answer: D. Infra-red thermometer

Guide: The intensity of infra-red radiation given out by an object increases when its temperature increases.

Q2 · JPHG01.1

Answer: D. (2) and (3) only

Guide: The lower fixed point is the ice point, not the lowest temperature in nature. The upper fixed point is the steam point. The unit is °C.

Q3 · JPHG01.2

Answer: A. 27.8 °C

Guide: Ice point is 0 °C and steam point is 100 °C. T/100 = (30 − 5)/(95 − 5).
