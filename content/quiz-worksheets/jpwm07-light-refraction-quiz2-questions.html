Q1 · JPWM07.3 · Foundation

The speed of light in a medium is 2 × 10⁸ m s⁻¹. What is the refractive index of the medium? (Given that the speed of light in vacuum = 3 × 10⁸ m s⁻¹)

  • A. 0.67
  • B. 1.33
  • C. 1.50
  • D. 2.00

Q2 · JPWM07.3 · Standard

When a light ray travels from air to water with an angle of incidence of 45°, the angle of refraction is 32.1°. What is the speed of light in the water? (Given that the speed of light in vacuum = 3 × 10⁸ m s⁻¹)

  • A. 2.14 × 10⁸ m s⁻¹
  • B. 2.25 × 10⁸ m s⁻¹
  • C. 2.42 × 10⁸ m s⁻¹
  • D. 2.59 × 10⁸ m s⁻¹

Q3 · JPWM07.4 · Standard

A boy is looking at small print on a piece of paper. He moves a glass block of uniform thickness 3 cm over the print. The print appears to rise by 0.9 cm. What is the refractive index of the glass?

  • A. 0.70
  • B. 1.30
  • C. 1.43
  • D. 3.33

JPWM07 Light refraction — Quiz 2 · Answer key

Teacher page · 3 items

Q1 · JPWM07.3

Answer: C. 1.50

Guide: n = c / v = (3 × 10⁸ m s⁻¹) / (2 × 10⁸ m s⁻¹) = 1.50. Option A is v / c.

Q2 · JPWM07.3

Answer: B. 2.25 × 10⁸ m s⁻¹

Guide: By Snell's law, n = sin 45° / sin 32.1° ≈ 1.33. Then n = c / v, so v = c / n = (3 × 10⁸ m s⁻¹) / 1.33 ≈ 2.25 × 10⁸ m s⁻¹.

Q3 · JPWM07.4

Answer: C. 1.43

Guide: Apparent depth = real depth − rise = 3 cm − 0.9 cm = 2.1 cm. n = real depth / apparent depth = 3 / 2.1 ≈ 1.43. Option A is 2.1 / 3. Option D uses the rise instead of the apparent depth (3 / 0.9).
