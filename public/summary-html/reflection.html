Rays, the laws of reflection, and images in a plane mirror

1

Light rays

Light is represented by rays.

A ray is a straight line with an arrow showing the direction light travels.

2

Light beams

A light beam is made up of a number of rays.

3

How we see things

Draw cones of rays from the tip and tail of the object.

Point both cones towards the eye and end them at the eye.

4

The laws of reflection

The incident ray, the normal and the reflected ray all lie in the same plane.

Angle of incidence = angle of reflection, i.e. i = r.

Seeing images

You see an image only if the line from the eye to the image passes through the mirror.

Real rays: solid with arrows. Virtual rays: dotted, no arrows.
