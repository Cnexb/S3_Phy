4. Power of ten notation (scientific notation)4. 十的次方記法（科學記數法）

Large or small numbers can be written using powers of ten. The index on 10 is the power of 10. 1 = 10⁰. Numbers less than 1 have negative indices. Match each ordinary number to 10n. 很大或很小的數可用十的次方表示。10 右上的指數就是十的次方。1 = 10⁰。小於 1 的數用負指數。把普通寫法配到 10n。

400 = 4 × 10²  ·  30 000 = 3 × 10⁴  ·  7 200 000 = 7.2 × 10⁶
0.05 = 5 × 10⁻²  ·  0.00002 = 2 × 10⁻⁵

5. Prefixes5. 前標

When a unit is too large or too small, use multiples or sub-multiples. These usually change by a factor of 1000 (sometimes 10 or 100). Match each prefix, then Rewrite. Tap a matched row to try that prefix. 當單位太大或太小時，用倍數或分數倍數。通常以 1000 倍增減（有時是 10 或 100）。先配對前標，再按改寫。點已配對的一列可套用該前標。

0.007 s = 7 × 10⁻³ s = 7 ms

1 000 m = 10³ m = 1 km  ·  1 000 000 V = 10⁶ V = 1 MV
0.01 m = 10⁻² m = 1 cm  ·  0.001 g = 10⁻³ g = 1 mg  ·  0.000 001 s = 10⁻⁶ s = 1 μs

6. Convert km/h to m s⁻¹6. 把 km/h 換成 m s⁻¹

The speed of a car can be expressed as kilometres per hour (km/h). × 1000 m / 1 km and × 1 h / 3600 s. Shortcut: divide by 3.6. 汽車速率可用千米每小時（km/h）表示。× 1000 m／1 km，再 × 1 h／3600 s。捷徑：除以 3.6。

(a) 36 km/h
(b) 50 km/h
(c) 100 km/h
