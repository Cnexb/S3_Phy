# Electromagnetic induction — practice questions

These are original practice items for the interview kit. They follow the SPEM07 brief. They are not past-paper extracts.

Use them to check the tool. A good tool can set up at least one of these, hide the answer until the student commits, and explain a wrong answer.

## Q1 — Flux

A single-turn coil has area 0.020 m². A uniform field of 0.40 T is perpendicular to the face of the coil (θ = 0°).

(a) Find the magnetic flux through the coil.

(b) The coil is turned so that θ = 60°. Find the new flux.

## Q2 — Faraday

The coil in Q1 has 200 turns. The flux through each turn falls uniformly from 0.0080 Wb to zero in 0.050 s. The coil stays at θ = 0° while this happens.

Find the magnitude of the average induced emf.

## Q3 — Faster change

The same 200-turn coil and the same flux change (0.0080 Wb to zero) now take 0.10 s.

(a) Find the new magnitude of the average induced emf.

(b) Why is it smaller than in Q2? Answer in one sentence.

## Q4 — Lenz

A bar magnet is held with its north pole facing a coil, then pushed toward the coil.

(a) Does the near face of the coil become a north pole or a south pole while the magnet approaches?

(b) The magnet is then pulled away, north pole still facing the coil. What does the near face become now, and why?

## Q5 — A wrong idea

A student says: “Induced emf appears whenever a magnet is near a coil, even if nothing is moving and the field is steady.”

Is the student right? Say why in one or two sentences.
